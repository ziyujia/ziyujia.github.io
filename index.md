# Biography

Dr. Ziyu Jia is an Associate Professor at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. **<font color='#1677D2'>He has long been engaged in research on non-invasive brain-computer interfaces and brain disease detection</font>**. His recent work **<font color='#1677D2'>has been published in leading computer science conferences such as ICML, NeurIPS, and KDD, as well as IEEE Transactions journals, with more than 80 papers in total</font>**. Over the past five years, his work has received more than 2900 citations on Google Scholar (H-index 30). He **<font color='#1677D2'>has led more than ten projects including the National Natural Science Foundation of China and National Key R&D Program projects</font>**, and has been granted 6 national invention patents and software copyrights. He serves as a council member of the Beijing Society of Brain Network and Brain-like Intelligence, **<font color='#1677D2'>an Associate Editor or Editorial Board Member for multiple Q1 Top journals including IEEE TAFFC and Information Fusion</font>**, and **<font color='#1677D2'>an Area Chair for conferences such as NeurIPS and IJCNN</font>**. He has been selected for the **<font color='#1677D2'>2025 MSRA StarTrack Program</font>**, the **<font color='#1677D2'>world's top 2% scientists list</font>**, and the **<font color='#1677D2'>CIE Young Talent Program</font>**. His work has made a positive and sustained academic impact in the interdisciplinary area of brain-computer interfaces and artificial intelligence.

<font color='red'>If you are interested in collaborating on topics such as Brain Foundation Models, Multimodal BCI, NeuroAI, and other related topics, I warmly welcome potential collaborators and research interns—feel free to reach out! Contact: +86 15810239891.</font>
